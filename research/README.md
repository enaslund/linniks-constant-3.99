These files are copied from the research repository in which the proof was developed. They are
dated records: each describes the state of the work when it was written, so statements about
what was public or private at the time, or about drafts of the paper, refer to that time. The
certificates, programs and reports they cite are in [`computations/`](../computations/README.md),
at the same paths. The paper ([`paper/linnik399.pdf`](../paper/linnik399.pdf)) is the
authoritative account of the proof.
